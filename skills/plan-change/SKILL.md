# Plan a Change

Validate the published issue contract and digest. Read applicable instructions, architecture, source, tests, and configuration. Produce ordered stable-ID steps mapped to issue acceptance scenario IDs, exact validation per step, risks, compatibility effects, and rollback. Preserve implementer discretion where the issue does not bind a choice. Stop on conflicts or missing product decisions. Do not edit or publish.
